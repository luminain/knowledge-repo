# Personas (User app)

These are working personas for the individual customer surface. Validate with real booking data before Phase 2 scope lock.

## P1 — Diaspora sender (“Send home / send abroad”)

- Lives in Kathmandu, Pokhara, or Chitwan; sending to family in Korea, Japan, UK, US, Australia, Gulf.
- Books from a phone, often after work.
- Cares about: pickup slot, tracking number to WhatsApp to the receiver, “has it left Nepal yet?”
- Pain today: desktop dashboard, retyping addresses, no push.

Primary MVP user.

## P2 — Receiver / family coordinator

- May not have an account.
- Needs public track-by-number and a simple status story.
- Will install the app if tracking + notifications are good; otherwise stays on WhatsApp screenshots.

MVP: guest tracking. Account optional.

## P3 — Repeat individual shipper

- Sends food, clothes, documents several times a year.
- Wants saved senders/receivers, last-shipment clone, and status noise only when something changes.

Phase 2.

## P4 — Small informal business using an individual login

- Exists in the wild even though this portal is “individual only”.
- Wants volume, invoices, multiple users.

Out of scope until a Business app ADR. Do not warp the individual app into a TMS.

## Anti-persona

- CSR / admin (they have / will have a different tool).
- Warehouse staff scanning bags.
- Guest who only wanted a website quote form.
